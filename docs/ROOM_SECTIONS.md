# New corners of the room

The user approved the existing look and requested these additions on 2026-09-06. Keep the blackberry palette, warm paper, phone-first layout, and existing bottom navigation. Letters belong in the physical world: do not add a postbox or digital-letter feature. The lyric wall is based on the handwritten lyric wall in her home. My Adventure Book takes scrapbook inspiration from _Up_, using original cover styling and artwork.

These destinations appear as objects near the bottom of Home and in the header's Explore shortcut. They have their own horizontally scrollable local navigation. All use the existing composer, image upload path, conflict versions, local store, and Django API store. No new sample lyrics or fabricated memories are inserted. Rabbit holes were removed entirely on 2026-09-24; the kind, entries, and events are gone.

## Watch nights

`#movie-night`, kind `movie_night`: an evening with a place, local calendar date/time, snacks, notes, picture, and up to eight film or series picks. It records whether Nooshin is watching **On my own** or **With Saman**. Places include Nooshin’s place, Saman’s place, Cinema, and Somewhere else. Pick titles manually or from the saved film collection. A choice can be selected explicitly or drawn from the shortlist; the chosen ID persists. Completed evenings require a chosen title and move to Watched. No tickets, invitations, messages, or external bookings are sent.

## My Adventure Book

`#adventures`, kind `adventure`: a stitched cover opens into a paged scrapbook. Each page holds a title, optional photo, story, place, date, and either Someday or A memory. Cover, previous/next buttons, page index, and filters work on phones and with a keyboard. One main image per page; add more pages for more photos. No proprietary _Up_ artwork is bundled.

## Wall of Lyrics

`#lyric`, kind `lyric`: user-entered words retain line breaks, song title, and optional artist, link, and photo. Choose parchment, rose, lavender, or midnight paper; handwritten, serif, or typewriter lettering; and three sizes. Arrange with drag/drop or labelled earlier/later buttons. Positions persist with the item's version check. Lyrics are never fetched or generated by the app.

## Little loves

`#little-loves`, kind `love`: one polaroid card per small love — image-centred, with a story (`why`, up to 700 characters) and up to 60 dated "little lines" that accumulate over time. Single status "Hers". Photos use the private media path; entries without one show a heart placeholder. No sample loves or lines are seeded.

## Moon days

`/moon-days`, kinds `cycle` and `body`: a cycle tracker for Nooshin. Each cycle entry is one period — first day (required), last day (optional; open means still going), flow (light/medium/heavy), and an optional note. The entry's title is derived from its dates, never typed. The page is built around a moon wheel: one tick per day of the cycle, period days in deep rose, today ringed, and the day counter plus the current phase in the centre. The wheel carries four faint phase bands — period, follicular, around ovulation, luteal — with the band she is in lit gold; ovulation is estimated 14 days before the next expected period, and an open period is assumed to run the usual five days. Entries whose logged span exceeds ten days are read as whole-cycle records (first day → the day before the next one began): only the period itself is drawn on the wheel and calendars, and history shows them as "N-day cycle" rather than one long period. Until she has logged periods the wheel runs on her usual 29 days; once two or more starts exist it turns on the average of the last six gaps (15–60 days kept), with a five-day expected window dashed after the wheel and an overdue count when it passes. A three-month strip below shows the same days on real calendars, plus a pattern card (usual length, phase, next expected date), and an editable history with per-cycle lengths. A weight card logs weigh-ins (kind `body`: weight in kg and a day, title derived, newest first with the change against the previous weigh-in). Her starting weigh-in (67.7 kg) was seeded 2026-09-24; she goes to the gym regularly, but no gym/exercise feature exists. This is sensitive health data: it stays behind member auth like everything else, is never predicted beyond the simple average, and sends no notifications.

## Data and rollout


Migration `0004_room_sections.sql` adds nullable `room_entries.details` and the four new kind/status contracts. Existing kinds remain compatible with null details. Client and SQL validation enforce typed metadata, valid calendar dates/times, bounded lists/text, allowed style values, and a chosen film or series belonging to the shortlist. Membership/RLS, signed attachments, events, and Realtime are inherited from the existing room tables.

Run `python backend/manage.py migrate` before deploying this frontend to Django. The `RoomEntry.details` JSON field validates the four section contracts; Django's member authorization, version checks, and private media handlers apply to every section. To revert the UI, deploy the previous frontend while retaining the Django schema and new entries. Back up any new entries before considering schema removal.

Validation during implementation: local browser create/edit/reload flows, image-backed adventure pages, movie selection and completion, wall styles/order, source rename/removal, invalid trail input, Axe WCAG A/AA checks, and 320/390/768/1024/1440px layouts. Django migrations, session membership, CSRF, optimistic versions, and private media were exercised against an isolated SQLite database; hosted PostgreSQL and provider credentials remain deployment checks.
